## Remaining limitations and next phase

The requested Phase 4 daily entry/report/chart work is implemented with real persistence. Actual iOS/Android device acceptance, native screen readers/gesture behavior, real low-storage/power-loss behavior, and the specification's large-dataset target remain unverified. Daily display rows are lazy, while report aggregation loads the selected and comparison periods into memory; whole-history performance is not certified. Product detail still loads all batches for that product. While open, local day changes are detected within 30 seconds; resume refreshes immediately.

Next planned feature work: **customer debt notebook workflows**, followed by portable backup/restore. The debt tab retains its honest foundation state, and Home never invents an outstanding amount. No daily record or profit is inferred from stock or debt, and daily writes do not modify those modules. Backups are unavailable in this build; records remain on this device.

Development IDs/signing and starter launcher icons remain. Web/desktop targets are preserved but unsupported by the mobile sqflite composition. See `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` for numeric/calendar bounds and integrity responsibilities, and `docs/DEPENDENCIES.md` for versions, licenses, and platform setup.
